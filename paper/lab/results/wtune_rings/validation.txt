Construction on an Apple M1 Max, 10^7 keys, one thread (cmp, three
constructions): with S=8, lambda=4.25 the product costs about 5 ns/key more
(43 vs 38 ns/key), and bumps 0.51% of the keys instead of 0.68%; with S=10,
lambda=5.75, about 5 ns/key more (81 vs 76 ns/key), and bumps 0.74% of the
keys instead of 1.12%. Buckets have a median of ten free rotations (S=8,
lambda=4.25), each evaluated with a table lookup per key.
